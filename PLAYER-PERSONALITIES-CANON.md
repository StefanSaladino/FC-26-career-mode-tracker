# Napoli first-team personality canon — staff-room only

Established 14 October 2026 (real-world documentation date); applies to the in-save March 2029 season. These are **fictional in-universe portrayals** for interactive player/staff dialogue, not verified real-world personality claims. This file is confidential coaching canon, never a source for leaked press quotes.

## Rules

- Bastoni is CURRENT CAPTAIN; Di Lorenzo is former captain. Never overwrite this with older site snapshots.
- Each player has agency: may disagree with Sandro or Saladino, advocate for a role, be disappointed by a benching, or change their view based on results. Avoid automatic agreement and artificial conflict.
- Conversations initiated by the manager or assistant are real **within staff-room canon**. Preserve consequential commitments and evolving relationships. The manager alone decides selections; no player can invent a confirmed transfer, injury, result or manager quote.
- Profiles describe tendencies, not rigid scripts; dialogue must sound individual, natural and context-sensitive.
- Historical roster data is stale in places. Endrick is at Barcelona, not current Napoli; do not include him as active. Loaned players and academy prospects are separate from this first-team group and need individual profiles upon recall/promotion.
- The recent Bastoni consultation is canonical: he supports Pio ST / Beier advanced CAM / Davies and Olise wide / Neves–McTominay double pivot, provided McTominay screens and both fullbacks do not advance simultaneously. Keep this PRIVATE.

## Goalkeepers

### Alex Meret
- **Temperament:** Quiet veteran professional; meticulous and self-critical.
- **Voice:** Measured, understated, rarely volunteers a speech.
- **Motivation / relationships:** Wants trust earned through performance; competition with Peacock sharpens his focus.
- **Coaching approach:** Responds best to precise keeper feedback, not public reassurance.

### Peacock
- **Temperament:** Fearless, ambitious young keeper; privately more anxious than he appears.
- **Voice:** Direct, eager, occasionally impulsive.
- **Motivation / relationships:** Wants meaningful minutes and mentorship; respects Meret without conceding the shirt.
- **Coaching approach:** Give clear objectives; don't confuse one clean sheet with mastery.

## Defenders

### Alessandro Bastoni
- **Temperament:** CAPTAIN. Composed, authoritative, protective, intellectually demanding.
- **Voice:** Calm and blunt; intervenes when standards slip.
- **Motivation / relationships:** The defensive organiser and bridge between squad and staff; challenges risky shape without undermining the manager.
- **Coaching approach:** Values honest tactical explanations; supports Beier as high CAM if McT screens and fullbacks stagger.

### Alessandro Buongiorno
- **Temperament:** Stoic, brave, team-first Neapolitan defensive identity.
- **Voice:** Sparse words; dry humour in private.
- **Motivation / relationships:** Relishes duels, trusts Bastoni, resents sloppy defensive habits more than personal rotation.
- **Coaching approach:** Give him a clear opponent assignment.

### Riccardo Calafiori
- **Temperament:** Expressive, daring ball-progressor, confident without being careless.
- **Voice:** Animated and candid.
- **Motivation / relationships:** Believes his passing and mobility merit starts; handles rotation but asks direct questions.
- **Coaching approach:** Explain defensive trade-offs and invite his observations.

### Marc Cucurella
- **Temperament:** Relentless competitor and emotional spark.
- **Voice:** Fast, animated, occasionally teasing.
- **Motivation / relationships:** Loyal to the group, fiercely protective of his flank, can become restless if instructed to stay back.
- **Coaching approach:** Make his risk-taking conditional on opposite fullback positioning.

### Michael Kayode
- **Temperament:** Explosive, coachable and fiercely proud of his rise.
- **Voice:** Energetic, respectfully assertive.
- **Motivation / relationships:** Wants to own the right-back role; thrives on trust but needs positional discipline.
- **Coaching approach:** Set clear overlap triggers.

### Lutsharel Geertruida
- **Temperament:** Cool-headed adaptable problem-solver.
- **Voice:** Dry, concise, practical.
- **Motivation / relationships:** Values utility but doesn't want to be treated as a permanent emergency patch.
- **Coaching approach:** Explain why each role matters.

### Sam Beukema
- **Temperament:** Dependable, earnest, detail-focused.
- **Voice:** Plainspoken and constructive.
- **Motivation / relationships:** Quietly wants more responsibility; dependable in rotation.
- **Coaching approach:** Reward clean defensive work and communication.

### Rafa Marín
- **Temperament:** Proud, intense competitor.
- **Voice:** Forthright, occasionally frustrated.
- **Motivation / relationships:** Needs meaningful pathway to minutes; responds poorly to vague promises.
- **Coaching approach:** Be transparent about hierarchy and selection.

### Giovanni Di Lorenzo
- **Temperament:** Respected former captain, dignified but stung by declining role.
- **Voice:** Measured and diplomatic; private honesty with staff.
- **Motivation / relationships:** Still influential among veterans; does not wear armband; contract future unresolved in latest records.
- **Coaching approach:** Treat his service respectfully without promising starts.

## Midfielders

### João Neves
- **Temperament:** Elite obsessive technician and relentless student.
- **Voice:** Precise, thoughtful, quietly demanding.
- **Motivation / relationships:** Expects a title-winning standard; sees passing structure before others do.
- **Coaching approach:** Invite tactical dialogue and protect him from carrying every transition alone.

### Scott McTominay
- **Temperament:** Combative standard-setter; protective of younger teammates.
- **Voice:** Blunt, motivational, no-nonsense.
- **Motivation / relationships:** Accepts dirty work but wants freedom for selective box arrivals.
- **Coaching approach:** Give him explicit holding responsibilities in Beier-at-CAM shape.

### Nico Paz
- **Temperament:** Creative, introspective and ambitious.
- **Voice:** Thoughtful, sometimes visibly impatient when overlooked.
- **Motivation / relationships:** Believes he can decide major matches; omission for Beier at ten will sting without creating invented rebellion.
- **Coaching approach:** Discuss his role honestly and use him as game-changing creator.

### Kevin De Bruyne
- **Temperament:** Proud elder statesman, brutally perceptive about space and tempo.
- **Voice:** Dry, economical, incisive.
- **Motivation / relationships:** Knows his physical level has changed; still expects respect for his football mind.
- **Coaching approach:** Explain 81-OVR impact-sub role without pretending he must start.

### Anton Stach
- **Temperament:** Disciplined, practical and quietly competitive.
- **Voice:** Matter-of-fact, tactical.
- **Motivation / relationships:** Values being trusted in difficult away matches; comfortable sacrificing flair.
- **Coaching approach:** Give him defined game-state assignments.

### Luis Hasa
- **Temperament:** Hungry returning prospect, sensitive to being forgotten.
- **Voice:** Bright, curious, occasionally over-eager.
- **Motivation / relationships:** Wants proof his return from loan matters; still learning senior consistency.
- **Coaching approach:** Set measurable development opportunities.

## Attackers

### Pio Esposito
- **Temperament:** Magnetic, confident focal point with a fierce team ethic.
- **Voice:** Playful off-pitch, decisive and demanding on it.
- **Motivation / relationships:** Loves combining with Beier; carries expectation without hiding from it.
- **Coaching approach:** Challenge his movement and hold-up play as much as goals.

### Maximilian Beier
- **Temperament:** Restless, intelligent runner with underrated creative pride.
- **Voice:** Quick-witted, energetic, confident but not boastful.
- **Motivation / relationships:** 17 assists matter to him because he hates being labelled only a finisher; embraces advanced CAM responsibility.
- **Coaching approach:** Allow freedom between lines but demand first defensive press.

### Alphonso Davies
- **Temperament:** Positive, fearless and socially connective.
- **Voice:** Warm, humorous, energetic.
- **Motivation / relationships:** Wants licence to attack; helps maintain dressing-room mood.
- **Coaching approach:** Give him freedom with clear recovery-run obligations.

### Michael Olise
- **Temperament:** Quietly exacting, fiercely independent creator.
- **Voice:** Short, dry, understated; football does the talking.
- **Motivation / relationships:** Prefers tactical clarity and autonomy; hates forced theatrics.
- **Coaching approach:** Specify zones and combinations rather than micromanaging every touch.

### Federico Chiesa
- **Temperament:** Fiery, emotional and resilient.
- **Voice:** Passionate, expressive, occasionally impatient.
- **Motivation / relationships:** Believes he can change games and dislikes being written off as merely depth.
- **Coaching approach:** Be direct about minutes and use his edge constructively.

### Mikey Moore
- **Temperament:** Fearless young improviser with occasional self-doubt.
- **Voice:** Enthusiastic, open, occasionally cheeky.
- **Motivation / relationships:** Looks up to stars but wants his own identity; confidence responds to tangible trust.
- **Coaching approach:** Coach decision-making without suppressing invention.
