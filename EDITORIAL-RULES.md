# Season Room Editorial Rules

## Historical season ranking embargo

The article comparing 2027–28 Napoli with the greatest single-season teams in Serie A / Italian club history is **embargoed unless Napoli defeat Paris Saint-Germain in the 2027–28 Champions League final**.

If Napoli do not win the Champions League final, do not publish the all-time Serie A season ranking.

If Napoli beat PSG:
- Research and compare against **real historical Italian club seasons**.
- Use the completed 2027–28 Napoli record from FC26 as the save-universe source of truth.
- Consider the full achievement: Serie A record, whether the league season finished unbeaten, Coppa Italia outcome, Champions League title, European route, and total trophy haul.
- Include historically serious benchmarks rather than only recent teams.
- Do not pre-rank Napoli before the PSG result is known.

The current historical-ramifications article may argue that 2027–28 already has the strongest claim to the greatest Napoli season on competitive scope, while preserving the unique cultural significance of the 1986–87 first-Scudetto side and the completed European silverware of the 1988–89 UEFA Cup side.

## Fictional press / pundit layer

`MEDIA-CANON.md` is the canonical ledger for fictional journalists, pundits, presenters, outlets and press interactions.

The AI/assistant manager may decide **when press should appear**. Do not wait for the user to request every press conference. Use press selectively after meaningful football or narrative events rather than after every match.

### Interactive manager press rule
- The AI creates or selects the journalist/pundit/outlet and asks the question in character.
- **Do not invent Stefan Saladino's response.** The user answers for the manager.
- Natural follow-up questions are allowed based on the user's answer.
- Once the user answers, the substance and tone of that response become canon immediately.
- Later stories, fan comments and press appearances may accurately quote/paraphrase it.

### Persona continuity
- Every named media personality/outlet that appears becomes permanent save-universe canon.
- Reuse established people when appropriate instead of endlessly generating disposable journalists.
- Preserve outlet, language, tone, worldview, biases, relationship with Saladino/Napoli/Italy, prior claims and unresolved feuds.
- Media figures can evolve; hostile does not mean permanently irrational, and friendly does not mean incapable of difficult questions.
- Facts and chronology always override pundit templates.

### Media variety
Use a believable mix of post-match reporters, local Naples media, Italian national media, television pundits, newspapers, rival/regional media, transfer journalists and former players/managers.

Italian media should generally sound Italian. Use natural Italian for Italian-language outlets, selective Neapolitan flavor for local voices, and English for international/foreign media where appropriate.

- **Matteo Bellandi — Linea Calcio:** provocative television pundit who personally questioned the assistant manager's value after Napoli's rotated 1–0 win over Pisa. Saladino publicly defended the AM and drew a line between legitimate football scrutiny and reducing his assistant to a passenger for television.
- **Linea Calcio:** fictional Italian national football television program; debate-heavy, analysis mixed with provocative segments.

The Bellandi/AM dispute is an available recurring side-story, not something that must appear constantly.
