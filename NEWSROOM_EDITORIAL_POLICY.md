# NEWSROOM EDITORIAL DIRECTIVE — 4 January 2030

**To:** Luca Ferraro, Media Director; Napoli Season Room editors and reporters
**From:** Assistant Manager Sandro Vitale, on the manager's instruction
**Status:** Immediate, standing policy

The publication lost its established player-interview format after the May 2029 Champions League final media day. This was an editorial failure. Restore it without compromising match-canon accuracy.

## Required practice
- Every confirmed match: factual recap, tactical context, continuity, curated supporter debate and genuine threaded replies. No invented scores, assists, minutes, injuries, lineups, xG, or results.
- Every **major** match or turning point: editorial desk decides whether to publish a mixed-zone reaction, player quote, press conference, or interview. Do not force a quote into every article.
- At least **one character-led feature per in-game month** while the season is active, with rotation across senior players, emerging players and squad members. If no credible occasion arises, record a deliberate editorial exception in the canon instead of silently omitting the format.
- Interview dialogue may be dramatized **only as explicitly labelled in-universe fiction**. Never present fictional quotes as authentic real-world testimony or as player words confirmed by the manager.
- Each player has a consistent but evolving personality. Questions must reflect actual career-mode events and previously established tensions.
- Maintain a single living CANON.md, with an editorial log and interview continuity notes. Record unresolved questions for later follow-ups.
- Before publication: verify article visibility, image existence/cropping, body depth, comments and replies, latest result, next fixture, and standings. Don't report visual verification without actually doing it.

## Retroactive package
Six interviews are backfilled across June, August, October, November, December 2029 and January 2030. These are retrospective fictional editorial pieces, not contemporaneous authentic transcripts. They do not establish new on-pitch events.

## Escalation
Any month lacking an interview/character feature must be flagged in CANON.md and repaired or explicitly explained at the next editorial review. No silent seven-month gap.
