## THE CURRENT ERA — CHAMPIONS OF ITALY, CHAMPIONS LEAGUE FINALISTS

SSC Napoli are 2027–28 Serie A champions and, for the first time in club history, UEFA Champions League finalists. **Paris Saint-Germain are the confirmed opponent in the Champions League final.**

### Current league state
Napoli have played 35 Serie A matches: **26 wins, 9 draws, 0 losses, 87 points**, with three league matches remaining. The unbeaten season remains alive. The latest league result was a heavily rotated **1–0 home win over Pisa**, Federico Chiesa scoring in the 11th minute from Endrick. Endrick later had a penalty saved.

### Historic Barcelona semifinal
First leg: **Barcelona 1–1 Napoli**. Barcelona scored early through Fermin; Scott McTominay equalised in the 22nd minute from Kevin De Bruyne. Alex Meret produced a huge second half.

Second leg at the Maradona: **Napoli 2–0 Barcelona**. Napoli advance **3–1 on aggregate**.
- **7′ Nico Paz — assist Maximilian Beier.** Alphonso Davies advanced the move before Beier supplied the credited final pass.
- Meret produced another immense performance, including a remarkable second-half double save.
- **75′ Federico Chiesa — assist Michael Kayode.** Chiesa's direct run and finish effectively ended the tie.
- Anton Stach and Giovanni Di Lorenzo were introduced late to help close the match.

This sends Napoli to the **first European Cup / Champions League final in club history**. Reaching the semifinal was already a club-best Champions League run; reaching the final extends the record.

### Champions League knockout gauntlet
**Inter eliminated → Real Madrid eliminated → Atletico Madrid eliminated → Barcelona eliminated → PARIS SAINT-GERMAIN IN THE CHAMPIONS LEAGUE FINAL.**

Inter: Napoli lost 2–1 at home, won 2–0 at San Siro, advanced 3–2 aggregate. Davies scored from Pio; Paz scored the aggregate winner from Endrick; Meret starred.

Real Madrid: Napoli won 1–0 at home and drew 1–1 away, advancing 2–1 aggregate. Bastoni and Buongiorno were central to surviving Madrid pressure.

Atletico Madrid: 0–0 away, 1–0 home, 1–0 aggregate. After 134 scoreless minutes across the tie, Pio Esposito scored in the 44th minute from Alphonso Davies. Atletico then laid siege; Meret preserved the tie.

Barcelona: 1–1 away, 2–0 home, 3–1 aggregate. McTominay scored from De Bruyne in the first leg. Paz from Beier and Chiesa from Kayode scored in the historic second leg. Meret was decisive across both matches.

No easy-path framing is legitimate. Napoli reached the final through a brutal sequence of elite opponents and repeatedly won ties in different ways. **PSG are the final obstacle.**

## Major player storylines

**Pio Esposito — OUR GUY / face of the era.** Pio remains the identity player of the Saladino era. His Atletico quarter-final winner is a genuine European legacy moment. He does not need to score in every major match to retain that status.

**Maximilian Beier — star, not supporting cast.** Twenty-two goals and now 11 assists. His pass for Paz in the Barcelona semifinal is another major European contribution.

**Nico Paz — long-term centerpiece.** His Barcelona seventh-minute goal is now one of the defining moments of his Napoli career, alongside the Inter aggregate winner. He scored the opening goal on the night Napoli reached their first Champions League final.

**Federico Chiesa — historic dagger.** Chiesa scored against Pisa and then scored the 75th-minute goal against Barcelona that made the semifinal 2–0 on the night and 3–1 aggregate. Six goals on the season after the Barcelona match.

**Alex Meret — European wall.** The redemption arc has become one of the central stories of the entire campaign. Atletico, Barcelona away and Barcelona home all contain defining Meret performances. The second-leg double save against Barcelona belongs in the permanent history of the run.

**Bastoni + Buongiorno — defensive foundation.** The partnership remains central to Napoli's ability to survive elite knockout pressure.

**Michael Kayode — fearless young gun.** Trusted in major matches; credited with the assist for Chiesa's historic Barcelona clincher.

**De Bruyne — veteran standard.** His experience and pragmatism matter increasingly as the season moves from celebration into final preparation.

**Di Lorenzo — old guard / captain.** His late introduction against Barcelona is part of the symbolism of the historic qualification: the old guard helping close the door on the way to a new frontier.
